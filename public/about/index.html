What we do

Quantum Affinity Gravity (QAG)

A theoretical framework for galactic dynamics and cosmology. The AVI law, tested on 175 SPARC galaxies, fits rotation curves with reduced-χ² 5.549 — against MOND's 56.554 and Newton's 616.742 — without dark matter halos. All figures are the team's own fits, not independently validated.

Offline-first edge AI

AI that runs on the device, not in a data centre — Aestheria (online grounding), AI-Offline (the offline mind), and Quanta AI (the merged, conversation-first app). Local-first and air-gapped by design.

Crypto tools

QACTS (crypto recovery + wallet dashboard) and Crypto Symbiote (mining companion) — software for research, monitoring and wallet management. You sign every transaction in your own wallet; we never ask for private keys.

How we work

  • Honesty over performance. The replication record is published with failures included. When a number doesn't reproduce, we stop and say so.
  • Open by default. Code, data and documents live in public repositories where they can be checked.
  • Local-first. Your data stays on your devices where it can. Cloud is a convenience, not a requirement.
  • Science as magic. The goal is to improve lives — starting with getting the physics right.
