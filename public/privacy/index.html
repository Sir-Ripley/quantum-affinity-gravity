Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · R&R Droids · Rodney A. Ripley Jr.

This policy covers quantumaffinitygravity.com and its subdomains (the "Sites"). We collect as little as possible, and we do not sell personal data — ever.

1. What we collect

  • Public sites (portal, docs, status, tools, data API): no account, no tracking cookies, no analytics profile of individual visitors. Our host (Vercel) keeps standard server logs (IP address, requested page, user agent) for security and operations, retained briefly and not sold or shared for advertising.
  • Applications with accounts (Nexus, Aestheria, Symbiote Cloud and similar): the email address and account details you provide, plus the data the app needs to function (course progress, device telemetry, mining status). Authentication is handled by Supabase; session tokens are stored in your browser so you stay signed in.
  • Contact: if you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect private keys, seed phrases, wallet secrets or payment details. Crypto tools never ask for them. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on the Sites, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

3. Cookies

The public Sites set no cookies of their own. Applications that require sign-in use a session cookie/token to keep you authenticated, and nothing else for tracking. Your browser's storage for those apps holds your session and local preferences only.

4. Third-party services

The Sites run on Vercel (hosting) and use Supabase (authentication and database) for the applications. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies and only as needed to run the service. The data API is CORS-open and serves public research data; requesting it does not create an account or profile.

5. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to export or delete your account data from the applications. Email research@quantumaffinitygravity.com and we will act on it. Public research data (SPARC fits, preprints) is published research and is not personal data.

6. Children

The Sites are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date above will change and the change will be noted on the portal. Continued use of the Sites after a change means you accept the updated policy.
